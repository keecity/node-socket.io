How it is built

Nine sculpts were split from one sheet, straightened by symmetry and aligned at the jaw joint. A generic base (the median of their shapes, so no species' sockets, horns or crests) is rigged with a skull and a jaw bone in the cheek and wrapped onto each sculpt, so splicing is a per-vertex blend on 28,142 shared vertices. What the base cannot reach (horns, frills, crests) is stored per species as a solid feature patch and fused on at incubation, scaled by that species' share of the genome.
