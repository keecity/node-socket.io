How it is built

Nine sculpts, painted with ID colours (pink mouth, yellow beak and dome keratin, green attachment sites), were split from one sheet, straightened by symmetry and aligned at the jaw joint. A generic base (the median of their shapes) is rigged with a skull and a jaw bone in the cheek and wrapped onto each sculpt, so splicing is a per-vertex blend on the shared vertices. Each species' ID regions are carried onto the base through its wrap and mixed by the same DNA, then cut at one half, so a beak or gum line moves with the genome without smearing. The ID colours never show: the skin is procedural.
