How it is built

The three sculpts were split from one sheet, straightened by symmetry, aligned at the jaw hinge and scaled to the same jaw length. A generic base was made from their averaged shape, rigged with a skull and jaw bone, then wrapped onto each sculpt. Every head shares the same 29,841 vertices, so splicing is a per-vertex blend and the jaw rig works on all of them.
